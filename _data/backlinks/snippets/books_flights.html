  • Recollections of the Spirit of Gravity
    The onrushing present has us trapped at the moment of disintegration. Lines of flight point in every direction, but they all bring us back to the same point: the ever-disintegrating, eternally-cresting, never-breaking Now. We would be better off writing all our wisdom down in a book—to retreat from society and laboriously teach the youth how to live outside of time. Memory has mass. We who can remember are weighed down by the Spirit of Gravity, while the force of forgetting flies weightless through the wild blue yonder. I chase that vivid perspective of my dream until reality and myth become one image. And I live by that first commandment: Never change!
