  • Recollections of the Spirit of Gravity
    An entire generation of the City sought in vain to bring History back to life by piecing together what it could remember. We could not achieve the weightiness of our youth, and now we are left to harvest chrysanthemums and radishes. They push up through the broken alley. Pebbles dig into the palm of my hand as I lean back, one night in that square kilometre. A crowd buzzes and smokes, white t-shirts glowing under halogen lamps. This was once the centre of my universe.
