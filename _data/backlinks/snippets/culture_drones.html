  • Recollections of the Spirit of Gravity
    In that square kilometre, an entire generation of the City sought in vain to bring History back to life by piecing together what it could remember. We could not achieve the weightiness we remembered, and now we are left to harvest our crop of chrysanthemums and radishes. They push up through the broken alley. Pebbles dig into the palm of my hand as I lean back, one night in that square kilometre. A crowd buzzes and smokes, white t-shirts glowing under halogen street lights. This was once the centre of my universe.
