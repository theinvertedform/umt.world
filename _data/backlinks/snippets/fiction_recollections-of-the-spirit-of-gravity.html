  • Recollections of the Spirit of Gravity
    I was walking home one night when an old man dressed in rags approached me. He reeked of the street and spoke continuously under his breath. I tried to proceed on my way, but he would not leave me be. The man was shrouded in darkness, his face obscured by a gnarled beard, but I perceived no threat, only desperation on his part. As we passed into the darkness of the bike path, I came to understand that his speech was a continuous parole; when he reached the end, he would just continue back again. Finally I understood that the sheaf of papers he held in his hand was for me, and it was not until I accepted them that he disappeared back into the gloom. This text, published erroneously in The Pit under my name, has now been significantly revised and posted online for your pleasure.
